What is inference?

Training is when a model learns its parameters.

Inference is when an already-trained model is used to produce an output.

Tokens
LLMs don't fundamentally process text as words. They process tokens. 
gets tokenized into a sequence of pieces. Tokens may correspond to whole words, parts of words, punctuation, etc.

Context window
Every LLM has a maximum amount of context it can process in a request.
